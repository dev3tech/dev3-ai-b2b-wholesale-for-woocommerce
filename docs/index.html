Sell to businesses and consumers from one WooCommerce store: wholesale roles with their own prices and buying rules, a fast quick-order table, and a complete request-a-quote workflow with PDF quotes that turn into orders. Optional DEV3 AI drafts quotes from a customer's own words and writes the emails. Every AI result is a draft you accept, edit or reject, and the plugin works fully without AI.

1. Installation

  1. Requirements: WordPress 6.0+, PHP 7.4+ (tested on 8.3), WooCommerce 8.0+.
  2. Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin → choose dev3-ai-b2b-wholesale-for-woocommerce.zip → Activate.
  3. A new menu DEV3 B2B & Quotes appears. Shop managers can use it too (filter dev3bq_manage_capability to change that).

The plugin declares compatibility with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS) and the Cart & Checkout blocks.

2. Quick setup (5 minutes)

  1. Settings → Pages: click Create page for the quote page, quick-order page and wholesale registration page, then save.
  2. Roles & tiers: two roles are created for you, Wholesale (10% off) and Distributor (20% off, $250 minimum). Edit or add your own.
  3. Pricing rules: add quantity breaks, for example “Distributor, all products, 20% off; 50+ 25% off; 100+ fixed $6”.
  4. Optional: API Config → add your Google Gemini key to turn on DEV3 AI.

3. Wholesale roles (tiers)

Each role becomes a real WordPress role (dev3bq_<key>) with customer capabilities. Per role you set:

Deleting a role turns its customers back into retail customers.

4. Registration and approval

Add the Wholesale registration block or [dev3bq_wholesale_registration] to a page. Applicants enter company, contact, tax ID, website, country, account type and a message. A honeypot field, same-origin check and rate limit stop bots.

  • Settings → Wholesale registration → Create the account: right away (as a retail customer until approved) or only when approved. WordPress emails the password link.
  • Wholesale customers → Pending: open an application, optionally screen it with DEV3 AI, pick the role (it can differ from the requested one), tax status and a note, then Approve or Decline. The applicant gets an email either way.
  • Customers tab: change a customer's role, tax status and internal note, or remove wholesale access.

5. Pricing

The price for a product, role and quantity is resolved in this order:

  1. Product-level price for the role (product edit screen → Wholesale tab, and on each variation), with its own quantity breaks written as 50:7.50, 100:6.
  2. Pricing rules: the highest priority matching rule wins (ties: the lowest price). A matching quantity break replaces the rule's base discount. Rules can target everyone, all wholesale roles or one role; all products, categories (sub-categories included) or products; with optional start/end dates.
  3. The role's default discount.

Discounts apply to the active price (the sale price when on sale). Quantity breaks count per cart line, or across all variations of a product (Settings → Quantity breaks count). Use the Price preview on the Pricing rules screen to check any product, role and quantity.

The product Wholesale tab also sets Who can buy (everyone, wholesale only, retail only), minimum quantity and pack size for wholesale buyers. Hidden products disappear from the shop, search, blocks and related products and return a 404.

Hide prices from guests: Settings → Prices for guests → Hide. Guests see “Log in to see prices” and can still request a quote.

6. Checkout rules

  • Minimum order value, minimum items, product minimums and pack sizes are enforced with woocommerce_check_cart_items, which the Store API also runs, so the block checkout is blocked too.
  • Payment methods per role and “Hide these payment methods from retail customers” (e.g. Invoice / net 30).
  • Shipping methods per role (by method type such as flat_rate or local_pickup) and wholesale-only shipping methods.
  • Orders store the buyer's role (_dev3bq_role) and show it in a “B2B” box on the order screen.

7. Quick-order table

Block Quick order table or [dev3bq_quick_order category="mugs" per_page="20"]. Customers search by name or SKU, filter by category, see their own price and quantity tiers, set quantities (rounded to minimums and pack sizes) and add everything to the cart or to a quote in one click. Paste a list instead turns lines like MUG-KRAFT-12 x 120 into selected rows.

8. Quotes

How customers request a quote

  • Add to quote on product pages (Settings → “Add to quote” button: everyone, guests, wholesale, only when the price is hidden, or off).
  • Request a quote for this cart on the cart page (classic and block cart).
  • The quote page ([dev3bq_request_quote]) shows the quote list, contact details and a “Describe what you need” box with an optional “Find these products” helper.

Working on a quote

Quotes → open a quote. Add products (priced by the rules for the chosen price list), type a price to override, add custom lines, shipping, an extra discount, tax mode, validity date, terms and a message. Re-price from rules refreshes every product line. Save, then Send quote: the customer gets an email with the PDF attached and a link to review it.

Statuses

StatusMeaning
New requestSubmitted by the customer.
In progressBeing edited by the store.
SentPriced and emailed; the customer can accept or decline.
AcceptedAn order was created.
Declined / Expired / CancelledClosed. Duplicate it to make a new version.

Accept → order

When the customer (or you, with Convert to order) accepts, a pending WooCommerce order is created through the CRUD API with exactly the quoted unit prices, shipping and discount, and the customer is sent to the WooCommerce “Pay for order” page. A quote can only be accepted once.

Reminders and expiry

A daily task sends one reminder N days after sending (Settings → “Send a reminder after”) and marks quotes past their validity date as expired (optional email).

PDF

The PDF is generated by the plugin (no external library) with your store name and address (Settings → Address on the PDF), lines, totals, message and terms. Currency symbols outside the PDF font are printed as the currency code.

10. Google Gemini API key

  1. Go to Google AI Studio and create an API key.
  2. In WordPress open DEV3 B2B & Quotes → API Config, paste the key and click Validate. The key is stored encrypted and never sent to the browser.
  3. Or define it in wp-config.php: define( 'DEV3BQ_GEMINI_API_KEY', 'your-key' );
  4. Default models: gemini-2.5-flash (fast) and gemini-2.5-pro. Optional: monthly budget, PII masking, storefront AI switch.
Privacy: data is sent to Google only when an AI feature runs: the request text and matching product names/SKUs, quote facts without addresses, or application details. A paragraph is added to your privacy policy guide, and quotes/applications are included in WordPress personal-data export and erase.

11. Emails

All emails are WooCommerce emails you can edit in WooCommerce → Settings → Emails, or override in your theme (woocommerce/emails/dev3bq-email.php and plain/dev3bq-email.php):

  • New wholesale application (store)
  • Wholesale application update: received / approved / declined (applicant)
  • Quote activity: new request, accepted, declined, customer message (store)
  • Quote request received (customer)
  • Quote ready, with PDF (customer)
  • Quote reminder / expired (customer)
  • Quote message (customer)

14. FAQ

Does it work with block themes and classic themes?

Yes, tested with Twenty Twenty-Five and Twenty Twenty-One.

Can AI change my prices?

No. AI picks products and quantities; prices always come from your pricing rules, and you review the draft before anything is saved.

Will page caching break the storefront forms?

No. Storefront endpoints do not depend on page nonces; logged-in shoppers' requests use their own session.

What happens to quotes when I uninstall?

Nothing, unless you enable Settings → Advanced → “Delete all data when the plugin is uninstalled”. Orders always stay in WooCommerce.
